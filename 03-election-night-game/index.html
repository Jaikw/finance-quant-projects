Upset math

Sips = round((1 − favorite's probability) × 20), minimum 1 when the underdog wins.
Example: a 71% favorite loses → round(0.29 × 20) = 6 sips. A 95% favorite loses → 1 sip. A 52% favorite loses → 10 sips.
